\section{Overview}

\noindent I founded the WEISER group at the National University of Singapore.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=4pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=4pt]
\item \textbf{Mentorship:} Currently advise \textbf{six} PhD students and \textbf{three} master's students. Graduated \textbf{one} PhD student and \textbf{sixteen} master's students, mentored \textbf{two} postdoctoral researchers, and supervised over \textbf{twenty} undergraduate students.
\item \textbf{Funding:} Secured over \textbf{1.2 million USD} across \textbf{seven} grants, including a Google Research Scholar Award and the ABB Research Award (300,000 USD)---the largest endowment by a commercial organisation for early-career researchers.
\item \textbf{Publications:} Published in flagship venues such as ACM MobiCom, MobiSys, SenSys, IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking, and IMWUT. Received a Best Paper Award and multiple Best Demonstration Awards.
\item \textbf{Industry Impact:} International Patent Application (Sole Inventor) cited as foundational prior art by \textbf{14+} patents from Nokia, Vivo Mobile, KAIST, and Oppo. Research featured in press releases by ABB and Google.
\item \textbf{Teaching:} Courses in embedded systems and wireless networking.
\item \textbf{Service:} Served as a reviewer and program committee member for top-tier international conferences in mobile computing, networking, and sensing (ACM MobiCom, SenSys, MobiSys, IEEE INFOCOM, ACM IMC). Organised and chaired sessions at major conferences with hundreds of attendees (ACM EWSN, ACM/IEEE IPSN, IEEE RFID).
\end{itemize}
